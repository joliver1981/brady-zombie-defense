🛒 Zombie Defense Shop

🧟 BRADY'S ZOMBIE DEFENSE 🧟

Rescue people! Earn 💵 💎 ♦! Build turrets! Survive the waves!

🎮 How to Play

  • WASD / Arrows — move
  • Mouse — aim, Click — attack
  • 1–6 — switch weapons
  • B — open the shop
  • N — start the next wave early
  • Walk up to caged people to rescue them 🧍
  • Rescues give 💵 money, 💎 diamonds and ♦ emeralds — and they join your squad and fight with you! (up to 3)
  • Loot 💰 buildings for big money and FREE turrets!
  • Upgrade your base in the shop — barbed wire, towers... all the way to a CASTLE 🏰
  • Buy a 🤖 Helper Robot and hand it a hammer or a bat!
  • Buy turrets and place them to defend your 🏠 base!
  • Buy costumes 🎩 for your turrets — they get +10% damage!

🧟 The Zombies

    🏜️ The Desert

    • 🌪️ The Dust Turret (7 ♦) pushes zombies back and can stun them!
